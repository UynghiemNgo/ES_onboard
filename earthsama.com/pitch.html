03 The Stakes

Microfinance as disaster prevention.

Strip a slope of native trees for yellow corn and it stops holding water. The hillside erodes, the rivers swell, and the valley floods.

  • Slopes stripped of trees — “particularly yellow corn farming” — drive the flooding
  • Financing the shift to agroforestry rebuilds the watershed that holds the soil
  • MFI makes that shift affordable for the farmer who can’t self-fund it

04 The Solution

We structure agricultural activity into trusted data.

An LGU-co-funded transition: get farmers off the deadly corn, onto safe livelihoods, and into the financial system — one verified record at a time.

01Onboard the farmer to a digital profile — the first time they exist to a financial system.
02Fund a $300 micro-facility, matched by a PHP 1,500 LGU co-fund, tied to financial-literacy milestones.
03Transition off corn into a native-tree nursery and a safe crop — keeping interim income, steepest plots first.
04Graduate the farmer to a real bank loan — de-risked, the bank pays us per opened account.

The same record that makes a farmer bankable today makes their land carbon-ready tomorrow — carbon is the upside, not the engine.

Computer vision + edge AI for field verification.

  • Built on prior computer-vision patents (US8593522, US8180158)
  • Growth mapping and object tracking for nursery + planting proof
  • Runs on cheap phones — low bandwidth, low light, offline

Verification is the lock between farmer and banker — proof the work happened, without a loan officer on the slope.

09 Moat 3 — Distribution

We don't chase farmers — institutions hand them to us.

🏛

The LGU wants corn off its deadly slopes. Its reforestation + agroforestry rollout is our distribution and our nursery buyer.
